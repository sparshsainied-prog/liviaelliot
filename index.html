All My Colours

She paints alone now. She used to paint with someone.

An enchanted ball jointed doll on milady Reine's shelves sees the world in colour, and captures it in watercolours to understand herself. The other dolls know little about her. Then one day, she makes a wish: for someone to see.

A standalone of about 150 pages, for readers of literary fiction, dark psychological stories and gothic fairytales.
